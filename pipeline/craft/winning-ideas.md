# What actually wins on this channel (measured, not guessed)

The judge reads this before picking anything. Updated 2026-09-05.

## The channel's own record

- **110K views, 2K likes, best email haul ever:** "The cure to AI slop is a
  1980s flight manual." 20 seconds, real face, free GitHub skill, comment
  GITHUB. Formula: [modern AI pain] x [obscure system from an unrelated
  industry] x [free asset] x [one-word CTA].
- "These 3 skills replace my $2k/month content team" - free skill suite,
  comment CONTENT.
- "Stop building AI agents. Do this first." - contrarian insider, from a
  person who sells agents for a living.
- The channel's ~1M+ impressions came from giveaways and contrarian-insider
  takes. **None of the winners were news coverage.**

## What wins for competitors (views/day, measured 2026-09-05)

- Model-launch reactions win HUGE but only same-day and only with a demo
  (Nate Herk 216K/day on GPT-6 Astra day one). A day late = dead.
- "Steal this" asset giveaways: evergreen compounding (130K total).
- Explainer-for-normal-people: 30K/day without any launch timing.
- Same-task head-to-heads ("I had both models build the same app"): 35K/day,
  no timing needed.
- Free multi-hour courses: the biggest long-form assets in the niche.

## The ownership gate (apply to every candidate)

Ask: **what does this video contain that no other channel gets from the
same feed?** Acceptable answers:
1. An asset WE ship (skill, repo, checklist, prompt) the viewer takes home.
2. A test WE actually ran, with our own recording of the result.
3. Insider translation only this creator can make (he runs an AI agency
   with enterprise clients and an automated content machine).
4. Same-day tier-1 launch WITH a runnable demo.

If the answer is "nothing, it is coverage", REJECT. A weak day with no
proposal beats a competent video 50 other channels can also make.

## Anti-patterns (all previously proposed, all rejected by the owner)

- Vendor speed/cost news with no operator action ("X tokens/sec on Y").
- "Company launched product" reframed as how-to without a real test.
- Any idea whose only receipt is someone else's benchmark.
